The Gallery: Selected Works and Case Studies

In the 3D gallery room, you can walk through an exhibition of Muhammad Moaiz's most significant commercial and personal projects, spanning private SaaS products, mobile apps, and premium storefronts.

RouteRadiant & NAMS Flow - Private SaaS Products

RouteRadiant is a private AI learning SaaS built around accessible, personalized education. NAMS Flow is a private customer communication SaaS for organized multi-channel business workflows. Both products are presented as public-safe case studies: learner and operator value is shown, while architecture, integrations, data models, and business logic stay protected.

Kippo & StockSuite - Social Media and Retail SaaS

Kippo is a private social media management SaaS for scheduling, analytics, and multi-account workflows. StockSuite is a private retail POS SaaS for inventory, sales, and store operations. Both focus on workflow clarity for real operators while keeping their internal systems intentionally non-public.

EduMate AI, BudgetO, SkyCast & SpotSpace - Public Mobile Apps

EduMate AI is a React Native AI course generator and study companion. BudgetO is a personal finance tracking app for expenses, income, and insights. SkyCast is a cross-platform weather forecasting app. SpotSpace is a full-stack parking reservation and facility management app covering both driver bookings and admin management.

NAMS Studio, Velvox, NemoDelivers & PakFiler

NAMS Studio is the agency's own Next.js website with motion-driven brand storytelling. Velvox is a premium men's fashion storefront concept. NemoDelivers is a food delivery experience focused on speed and trust. PakFiler is a protected Pakistani filing SaaS with guided user workflows.
